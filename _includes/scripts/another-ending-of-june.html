Another Ending of June

Character: A, B

INT. ROOM - AFTERNOON

The room bears traces of two lives — one side neat and minimalist, the other slightly messy. It’s an overcast afternoon. The light is dim and heavy. A sits by the window, looking out at the vast ocean. She has been staring out for a long time.

B enters the frame, stopping a short distance from A.

B (Softly) You’re alright?

A turns, looks at her, not in the eyes.

A Yeah, I’m fine.

They are clearly very familiar. This conversation seems to have played out countless times, each without resolution.

EXT. BEACH / GRASSLAND - DAY

A and B walk together. In the foreground, tall grasses sway; the ocean is visible behind them. B walks steadily, while A skips, kicks sand, runs around.

She stops mid-spin, looking out at the sea. She is silent for a moment.

A Do you think the end of the sea is still the sea?

B crouches down beside her, silently looking at the ground. After a moment, she looks up at the ocean.

B Maybe. Books say all the water in the world will meet again.

A puts her hands on her hips, lets out an "Oh," and says:

A That sounds pretty sweet.

Suddenly, she breaks into a run. B stays crouched, watching her fade into the distance.

A turns back midway, her gaze falling somewhere — maybe on B, maybe on the sea.

B sees her lips move, but can’t hear what she says.

(Cut to black. If subtitles appear...)

A (V.O.) I want to see the burning house.

INT. ROOM - NIGHT

A and B are inside. A lounges casually on a chair, reading. She has a cigarette held between the fingers of her left hand, and occasionally writes something down with her right. B sits on the floor by the bed, writing her homework. The room is bathed in a warm glow from a candle lit on the desk.

A looks out the window. Flames flicker outside, their light dancing across her face.

In her pupils, the fire trembles. She seems confused, and recognized.

The ticking sound of a clock fills the room, marking the passage of time. Tick. Tick. Tick.

EXT. UNDER THE TREE - DAY

A and B sit under a massive, ancient tree. It is tall, huge, and thick-trunked. A gets up and hugs the trunk, looking up into the branches and leaves.

A How do you think a tree processes sadness? How has it existed for so long, as if it will last forever?

B subtly furrows her brow and looks up as well.

B Maybe it doesn’t deal with it at all. It just records it — in its rings. Everything that happens is both part of it and apart from it. It just... exists.

A rests her cheek against the rough bark, closing her eyes. She looks simultaneously happy and sad, for the existence of the tree, or maybe existence itself.

A (Whispering) I wish I could exist like that.

She suddenly makes a funny face, sprints to B, and shouts.

A (Loudly) Boo!

B cooperates, falling backward, pretending to be startled. A laughs and runs off. B sits up, smiling as she watches A go.

INT. ROOM - DAY

Rain streaks the windowpane outside. A is pressed against the glass, looking out with curiosity. B walks up behind her. A pouts.

A The sky is soooo emotional. Cries every day. I don’t want to go to class today.

B smiles, but says nothing.

The sound of time passing. Tick, Tick, Tick…

INT. ROOM - EVENING

A is alone. It is twilight, and the lights are off. The room is very dim. A looks profoundly weary. She stares out the window for a moment, then decides to light a candle.

She strikes the lighter — click. Click. Click.

On the third try, a small flame flickers alive.

She lights the candle, watching the flame dance.

She pulls out a cigarette, lights it with the candle.

For a moment, she smiles faintly — she’s never lit one this way before.

Time passes.

The ticking grows louder.

She traces faint lines along her left arm with a knife.

Faded scars lie beneath her skin.

She inhales, exhales.

Then, without emotion, she cuts down.

Blood rises.

Silence.

Or maybe just the echo of something ending.

INT. ROOM / HALLWAY - NIGHT

B returns home. It’s late. She stands in the doorway, gripping the bag tightly.

In the faint light inside, only her silhouette is visible.

SUBTITLE LET'S SEE THE BURNING HOUSE.

SUBTITLE (A moment later) OKAY.

EXT. STREET - NIGHT (DREAM)

Two black figures run hand-in-hand through a street that feels both endless and familiar. The sides are lined with the shadows of grass and trees, pitch black yet eerily alive, like another world.

The air hums, thick with something unspeakable.

They stop.

A house burns in silence — no crackle, no scream, only light.

The flames move slowly, almost backwards, folding into themselves.

SUBTITLE: Why is it burning?

SUBTITLE (after a beat): It just is.

The flames flicker — and the scene shatters into white.

END.
